# Field Service Knowledge Base

This is the reference material the assistant is allowed to answer from. If a
question is not covered here, the assistant must say it doesn't know — it must
not guess.

---

## 1. Unit Reset Procedure (Cooling Units — CU series)

To perform a standard reset on a CU-series cooling unit:

1. Switch the unit to **OFF** at the main panel and wait **60 seconds**.
2. Hold the **RESET** button for 5 seconds until the status LED blinks amber.
3. Switch the unit back to **ON**. The LED should turn solid green within 2 minutes.
4. If the LED stays red after 2 minutes, the reset failed — escalate to a supervisor.

**Do not** perform more than 3 consecutive resets; repeated resets can trip the
compressor lockout.

---

## 2. Safety Lockout (Lockout/Tagout)

Before servicing any powered equipment:

1. Identify all energy sources for the unit.
2. Shut off power at the disconnect and apply your personal lock and tag.
3. Verify zero energy with a meter before touching internal components.
4. Only the person who applied a lock may remove it.

Never bypass a lockout, even if another technician asks you to. If you find a
unit locked out by someone else, do not service it — escalate.

---

## 3. Warranty Rules

- Parts are covered under warranty for **12 months** from the install date.
- Labor is covered for **90 days** from the install date.
- Warranty is **void** if a non-approved part was previously installed, or if the
  unit shows signs of unauthorized modification.
- Warranty claims require the original work order ID and the asset serial number.

---

## 4. When to Escalate

Escalate a work order to a supervisor when:

- A reset fails (status LED stays red) — see section 1.
- You discover a safety hazard (exposed wiring, gas smell, water near electrical).
- The required part is out of stock or back-ordered.
- The customer disputes the warranty status.

Escalation should include a short reason so the supervisor has context.

## 5. Standard Filter Replacement (AF series air filters)

1. Power down the unit and confirm airflow has stopped.
2. Open the filter housing; note the airflow-direction arrow on the old filter.
3. Insert the new AF-series filter with the arrow pointing toward the blower.
4. Close the housing and restore power. Log the replacement date on the work order.

Filters should be replaced every 90 days under normal use, or every 30 days in
high-dust environments.
